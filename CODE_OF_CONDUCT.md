# Code of Conduct

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Treat people respectfully. Discuss ideas and evidence, accept constructive
feedback, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention,
deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns privately to the maintainer using the email address on the [portfolio contact page](https://marinasofia.github.io/#s06)
with the subject `Community conduct`. Include the relevant context and links,
and share only the personal information needed to investigate. Do not file a
public issue containing sensitive allegations or personal information.

The maintainer will review reports, seek clarification where needed, and
limit disclosure to what is necessary to address the incident. Depending on
severity and repetition, responses may include a request to correct behavior,
content removal, a warning, or temporary or permanent exclusion. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant is unacceptable. A request to reconsider a
decision can be sent privately with new context.
